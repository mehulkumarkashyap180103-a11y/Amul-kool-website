Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions & Tips

How should I store Amul Kool Rose before opening? expand_more
Thanks to Amul’s advanced UHT (Ultra High Temperature) aseptic processing and multi-barrier hermetic sealing, unopened bottles can be safely stored at ambient room temperature in a cool, dry place for up to 180 days. However, for the true royal experience, we highly recommend chilling it in the refrigerator at 4°C for at least 2 hours before drinking.
Is Amul Kool made with real fresh milk? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. Amul Kool contains 100% standardized pure cooperative dairy milk. Unlike powdered recreations or oil-stabilized non-dairy drinks, Amul Kool retains natural dairy nutrients including complete milk protein, vitamin B12, and bio-available calcium.
Can Amul Kool Rose be used in desserts or served warm? expand_more
While designed to be enjoyed ice-cold, it is a versatile culinary delight! You can use it as a flavorful base for royal Rose Kheer, pour it over chilled Falooda vermicelli, blend it into rose ice-cream smoothies, or gently warm it during cold winter evenings for a calming floral nightcap.
Are there any added artificial preservatives in this drink? expand_more
None. Zero chemical preservatives like sodium benzoate or potassium sorbate are added. The drink’s shelf-life is achieved strictly through thermal aseptic sterilization and hermetic barrier packaging, guaranteeing pure and wholesome indulgence.
